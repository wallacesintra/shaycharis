About us

Every journey starts with proper planning.

Shay Charis Travel Solutions is an emerging travel-solutions business in Kenya. We help individuals, families, groups and organizations plan journeys that are convenient and memorable.

We help you explore destinations, accommodation options, itineraries and travel arrangements that match your interests and your budget. Clear communication and an understanding of each traveller's needs come first, so the relationships we build can last.

Our vision

To become a trusted travel-solutions brand recognized for excellent customer service, personalized travel experiences, reliability and professionalism.

Our mission

To help clients plan travel that suits their needs and budgets through personalized guidance, thoughtful coordination, transparent communication and responsible service.
